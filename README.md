# WeatherTS

**WeatherTS is a free desktop weather workstation built for meteorologists, forecasters, storm chasers, researchers, students, and weather enthusiasts.**

It brings radar, satellite imagery, atmospheric analyses, soundings, observations, weather alerts, outlooks, and pressure charts together on a single interactive map and timeline.

Instead of switching between multiple websites and tools, WeatherTS provides a unified workspace for exploring weather data, monitoring conditions, and analyzing the atmosphere.

**No paid tier. No locked features. No usage limits. No account required to use the app.**

## What You Can Explore

### Radar

WeatherTS provides detailed access to the **NEXRAD radar network**, including:

* Level II and Level III products
* Super-resolution Level II data
* Multiple radar tilts and elevation cuts
* Dual-polarization products
* Hydrometeor classification
* Echo tops, VIL, precipitation accumulation and more
* Storm tracks and forecast positions
* Hail, rotation and TVS information
* VAD wind profiles and hodographs
* Historical radar playback
* Animated radar loops

Radar data is available across the NEXRAD network, including sites in the United States, Puerto Rico, Hawaii, Alaska, Guam, Korea, and the Azores.

### Satellite

Explore satellite imagery from around the world, including:

* GOES-East and GOES-West
* Himawari
* Meteosat
* Metop
* NOAA-20 and NOAA-21
* Suomi NPP
* Terra and Aqua
* Sentinel-3
* Global satellite mosaics

With **96 satellite products**, WeatherTS includes visible, infrared, water vapor, RGB composites, lightning, and other derived products.

Satellite imagery can be animated across time, with loops ranging from approximately one hour to seven days.

### Atmospheric Analysis

WeatherTS includes a wide range of atmospheric analysis products from **HRRR, RAP, and RTMA**, including surface and upper-air fields as well as severe-weather parameters.

Explore products such as:

* CAPE
* SRH
* STP
* SCP
* Surface conditions
* Upper-air analysis
* Winter weather parameters
* Wind fields
* Temperature and moisture fields
* And more

Analysis products can be viewed through historical timelines with multiple days of available frames.

### Soundings & Observations

WeatherTS brings observational and sounding data into the same workspace.

Available data includes:

* Global METAR observations
* Radiosonde soundings
* Model point forecasts
* ACARS aircraft profiles
* Sounding analysis and comparison tools
* Trend and risk views
* Custom sounding uploads
* Hodographs and atmospheric profiles

### Weather Alerts & Outlooks

Monitor severe weather information through integrated alerts, reports, and forecast products.

WeatherTS currently includes:

* NWS warnings, watches, and advisories
* VTEC-decoded alerts
* Local storm reports
* SPC mesoscale discussions
* WPC precipitation discussions
* SPC Day 1–5 outlooks
* WPC excessive rainfall outlooks
* CPC extended outlooks
* NadoCast severe weather probabilities

### Pressure & Model Data

WeatherTS includes pressure and thickness charts from multiple major numerical weather prediction sources.

Currently available products include sea-level pressure and 1000–500 mb thickness from models and agencies including:

* NOAA
* ECMWF
* DWD
* Met Office
* Environment Canada

## A Workspace Built for Weather Analysis

WeatherTS is designed around a shared map and timeline, allowing different types of weather information to be viewed together.

You can:

* Use up to **six panels simultaneously**
* Compare different products side by side
* Animate data through time
* Switch between multiple basemaps
* Use vector, imagery, terrain, and globe views
* Measure distances, areas, and radii
* Search for locations
* Switch between light, dark, and automatic themes
* Export maps as PNG
* Export animations as GIF or MP4

The goal is simple: **put the information you need for weather analysis in one workspace.**
