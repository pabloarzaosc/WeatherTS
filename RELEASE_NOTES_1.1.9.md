## WeatherTS 1.1.9

Seven observed-data products, each reading the radar and the surface directly.

- **Interactive soundings** — skew-T plotting driven from the map.
- **ACARS** — aircraft-relayed temperature and wind profiles on ascent and descent.
- **Area-averaged sounding** — draw a box, get one profile averaged over it.
- **Local storm reports** — NWS spotter reports, shaped and placed by hazard.
- **VAD Wind Profile (VWP)** — the radar's own wind profile, with a hodograph and kinematics.
- **Surface fronts / boundaries** — WPC coded bulletin, drawn with proper front pips.
- **Storm tracks** — SCIT cells with past and forecast tracks, and a cell readout on click.
